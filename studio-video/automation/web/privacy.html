Privacy Policy

Aldi Studio · Last updated 18 November 2026

This policy explains what Aldi Studio (https://studio.aldiab.ai.id), run by Aldi AB, collects when you use it, and how that data is used.

What we collect

  • From TikTok, when you sign in: your TikTok open ID, display name and avatar (scope user.info.basic), and the access tokens needed to post the videos you choose (scope video.publish). We do not read your followers, messages, or other people's data.
  • What you create: your ideas, prompts, storyboards, captions, the generated narration audio and the rendered videos.
  • Usage records: AI tokens and render time per video, used for your monthly limit and invoices.
  • Technical data: a sign-in cookie that keeps you logged in, and basic server logs.

How we use it

Only to run Aldi Studio for you: to sign you in, produce your videos, post them to your TikTok account when you press Post, show your usage, and invoice you. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

Who processes it

Your prompts and storyboards are sent to Anthropic's Claude to write scripts and animation code, and narration text is sent to a text-to-speech service. Videos you post are sent to TikTok. These providers process the data only to deliver those functions.

Storage and security

Data is stored on Aldi AB's own server. Tokens are never shown on any page. Each creator can see only their own videos.

Retention and deletion

We keep your videos and usage records while your account is active, and usage records as long as needed for invoicing. To delete your account and videos, email studio@aldiab.ai.id. You can also remove access at any time in the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services; the stored token then stops working.
