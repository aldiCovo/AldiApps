Privacy Policy

Aldi Studio · Last updated 18 November 2026

Aldi Studio is a private tool used only by its owner to make short videos and post them to the owner's own accounts. It has no other users and collects no data from the public.

What we receive from TikTok

When the owner connects a TikTok account, TikTok gives Aldi Studio the permissions user.info.basic, video.upload and video.publish. Aldi Studio stores only the token needed to post videos. It does not read followers, messages, or other people's data.

How data is used

The token is used only to upload and publish the owner's videos to the owner's TikTok account. Nothing is sold or shared with third parties, and nothing is used for advertising.

Storage and security

The token is kept on the owner's own server, behind a login, and is never shown on any page.

Removing access

Access can be removed at any time from the TikTok app under Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services. The stored token then stops working, and the owner deletes it from the server.

Changes

This policy may change. The date above shows the latest version.
